What We Build

Local tools with global standards

Every product we ship is built on three uncompromising principles: your data stays on your device, every interaction feels intentional, and every detail respects your attention.

Privacy by Design

No accounts, no cloud sync by default, no behavioral tracking. Your personal data lives only on your device, encrypted and under your control — always.

Native Excellence

Built with native iOS and Android toolchains. We sweat the milliseconds, the gestures, the haptics — so the experience feels right at home on your platform.

Glass & Neumorphic UI

We blend glassmorphism, neumorphism, and platform-native aesthetics with smooth motion and tactile feedback — turning utility into a quiet pleasure.

Data Visualization

We turn scattered personal parameters into clear, visual stories — so you can see patterns, weigh options, and feel in command of your own time.

Modular by Default

Every product is a kit of composable modules. Customize categories, rearrange features, tune workflows — the tool bends to your life, not the other way around.

Global Compliance

GDPR, CCPA, LGPD, COPPA — built to the strictest standards from day one. Compliant on the App Store, Google Play, and every major ad & IAP platform.
