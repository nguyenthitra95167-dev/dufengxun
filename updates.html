Recent

Latest from Dufengxun

A chronological log of releases, milestones, and writing. We update this page as we ship.

September 2025

PulseTrack 3.0 — Native iOS Refresh

Complete SwiftUI rewrite, new radial visualization engine, and on-device encrypted backups. Ships with an updated privacy nutrition label and full GDPR / CCPA / LGPD compliance documentation. Available now on the App Store.

August 2025

Privacy Manifest Disclosure Pass

We completed an internal audit of every active Dufengxun app to ensure all Required Reason API usage is disclosed per Apple's Privacy Manifest policy. Updated manifests are live in every product build.

July 2025

DecideWise — Android Launch

Following the iOS launch, DecideWise lands on Google Play with full Material 3 theming, dynamic color, and the same on-device data architecture. Features weighted scoring, decision trees, and multi-criteria analysis.

June 2025

Ad Mediation Refresh — ATT-Consent Aware Bidding

We've migrated all IAA inventory to consent-aware bidding with Google AdMob, AppLovin MAX, and Pangle. Users in ATT-restricted regions see contextual-only ads, fully aligned with Privacy Sandbox guidance.

May 2025

ISO 27001 & ISO 27701 Recertification

Our information security and privacy information management systems have been recertified. The full Statement of Applicability and audit reports are available to enterprise partners on request.

April 2025

ChronoFlow 2.5 — Liquid Animation Engine

ChronoFlow picks up a new animation engine built on Core Animation (iOS) and Compose MotionLayout (Android). The clock face now responds to scroll velocity with physically-modeled damping.

March 2025

SpecKit — Modular Categories Released

Users can now define, rearrange, and share custom category systems in SpecKit. Modules are exported as portable JSON, fully under user control — no server sync, no analytics.

February 2025

MoodCanvas — Multi-Modal Input

Mood entries now support voice notes, color tagging, and the standard 1–10 scale. All processing remains on-device, including on-device speech-to-text via the system framework.

January 2025

StockMate — Barcode & Expiry Workflows

StockMate ships with a redesigned barcode scanning experience and a new expiration alert workflow. Camera frames never leave the device — recognition runs through AVFoundation and ML Kit's offline mode.

December 2024

Annual Privacy Report Published

Our second annual transparency report is out — covering all data requests received, government inquiries, takedown requests, and the total volume of telemetry we received from our apps in 2024. Spoiler: zero. Read the full report on our updates page.
