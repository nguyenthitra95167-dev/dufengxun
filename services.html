Track 01 — Our Products

Apps we ship under our own label

Every product in our portfolio is conceived, designed, and built by our in-house team — published globally on the App Store and Google Play, monetized through IAA ads and IAP, and engineered to handle the strictest privacy regulations out of the box.

How our apps monetize — transparently

IAA — In-App Advertising

Ads are served through globally compliant networks: Google AdMob, Google Ad Manager, Meta Audience Network, Unity Ads, AppLovin, ironSource, Digital Turbine, Chartboost, Vungle, Pangle (by ByteDance), InMobi, Verizon Media, Smaato, Start.io, Tapjoy, AdColony, Mintegral, Liftoff, Moloco, and platform-native Apple Search Ads / iAd. Formats: banner, interstitial, rewarded video, native, app open, MREC. All ad SDKs are configured for ATT, Privacy Sandbox, GDPR TCF v2.2, and CCPA opt-out signals.

IAP — In-App Purchase

Subscriptions, lifetime unlocks, and consumable features are processed through Apple App Store In-App Purchase, Google Play Billing, and RevenueCat for unified cross-platform receipt validation. Refund, subscription management, and family-sharing flows are fully implemented per platform guidelines.

Compliance Stack

Every shipped app is built against Apple's App Store Review Guidelines, Google Play Developer Policy, ATT Framework, Privacy Sandbox, GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, COPPA, China's PIPL, and the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework. SDKs are versioned, allow-listed, and disclosed in every product's privacy nutrition label.
